## About Me

I'm an ECE undergrad at **NIT Jalandhar** who ended up spending more time in PyTorch than in circuit labs. I like taking things apart — a GPT2 model, a login flow, a rate limiter — and rebuilding them from first principles so I actually understand the guts.

Right now I split my time between:

- 🧠 Building ML systems from scratch (yes, including the transformer math)
- ⚙️ Backend + distributed systems — the boring-sounding stuff that makes everything else work
- 🔐 Enterprise integrations (OAuth2, REST APIs) after a stint at **Accenture** wiring up Workday
- 🏆 Grinding LeetCode — 500+ down, no signs of stopping
